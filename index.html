Section 1

Personal Hygiene

Personal hygiene means keeping your body clean and healthy to prevent infections, unpleasant odors, and diseases while boosting confidence and well-being.

Clean Body + Clean Clothes + Clean Habits

= Better Hygiene & Better Health for Life!

Prevent Germ Spread

Stops the transmission of harmful bacteria, viruses, and pathogens causing daily infections.

Skin & Hair Care

Removes environmental pollutants, dead skin cells, and sweat accumulations effortlessly.

Dental Protection

Prevents cavities, gingivitis, tartar buildup, and premature tooth decay.

Odor Control

Eliminates sweat-loving bacteria responsible for body and mouth odor throughout the day.

Physical Health

Supports robust immune system performance and active daily physical energy.

Self Confidence

Enhances personal comfort, social interaction assurance, and positive mental well-being.

01

Bathe Regularly

Take a bath or shower regularly, especially after exercising or heavy sweating. Pay special attention to areas prone to sweat accumulation such as underarms, neck, feet, and skin folds.

  • Clean skin folds and underarms thoroughly
  • Rinse off all soap residues completely
02

Wear Clean Clothes

Change your clothes daily or immediately after sweating. Wash undergarments frequently and ensure all garments are completely dry before wearing to avoid fungal growth.

  • Wear breathable cotton fabrics
  • Ensure clothes dry thoroughly in sunlight/heat
03

Keep Your Skin Clean

Wash away daily sweat, environmental dirt, and excess sebum. Use mild skin-cleansing products suitable for your skin type and avoid harsh chemicals that cause irritation.

  • Moisturize skin after bathing
  • Avoid harsh synthetic soaps
04

Take Care of Your Feet

Wash your feet daily and dry them thoroughly, particularly between the toes. Wear clean, dry socks and ensure your footwear is given time to air out between uses.

  • Dry thoroughly between toes
  • Change socks daily

1. Brush Twice Daily

Brush your teeth for at least 2 minutes in the morning and before bed using fluoride toothpaste.

2. Clean Your Tongue

Gently clean your tongue with a scraper or brush to remove odor-causing bacteria.

3. Floss Regularly

Remove trapped food particles and plaque from interdental gaps where brushes cannot reach.

4. Hair Care

Wash hair according to your specific hair type and sanitize combs and brushes regularly.

5. Nail Care

Keep fingernails and toenails clean and trimmed neatly. Avoid biting nails or picking cuticles.

6. Personal Grooming & Skin

Groom facial/body hair clean and apply suitable moisturizer to maintain skin moisture barrier.

Diseases & Their Cure

Learn key symptoms, preventive measures, and recommended medical care for common illnesses.

Common Cold & Flu

Viral Infection

Symptoms

  • Runny or blocked nose
  • Frequent sneezing
  • Sore throat & irritation
  • Persistent dry or wet cough
  • Mild headache & tiredness
  • Low-grade fever (occasionally)

Prevention

  • Wash hands regularly with soap
  • Cover mouth & nose when coughing/sneezing
  • Avoid touching eyes, nose, or mouth
  • Avoid close contact with sick individuals
  • Disinfect frequently touched surfaces

Treatment & Care

There is usually no specific antiviral cure for the common cold. Most individuals recover naturally within 7–10 days.

Ample Rest Hydration & Warm Fluids Symptom Relief
Note: Antibiotics kill bacteria and do NOT treat viral colds or flu.

Dengue Fever

Mosquito-Borne Virus

Symptoms

  • Sudden high fever
  • Severe headache & retro-orbital pain (behind eyes)
  • Intense muscle and joint pain ("breakbone fever")
  • Nausea, vomiting & loss of appetite
  • Skin rash & extreme fatigue

Prevention

  • Apply mosquito repellents (DEET/Picaridin)
  • Wear full-sleeved shirts and long pants
  • Use window screens and bed nets
  • Eliminate standing water to stop mosquito breeding
  • Keep living surroundings clean and dry

Treatment & Care

There is no specific antiviral treatment for Dengue. Care focuses on fluid replenishment, rest, and close medical monitoring.

⚠️ Severe Dengue Warning Signs:

Severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, mucosal bleeding, extreme weakness, or difficulty breathing require immediate emergency medical attention.

Obesity

Metabolic Condition

Risk Factors

  • Excess calorie intake over long periods
  • Sedentary lifestyle & lack of physical activity
  • High consumption of ultra-processed foods
  • Poor sleep quality & chronic stress
  • Genetic, endocrine & environmental factors

Prevention & Habits

  • Consume a nutrient-dense balanced diet
  • Engage in 150+ mins of physical activity weekly
  • Stay well-hydrated throughout the day
  • Maintain consistent, healthy sleep schedules
  • Make gradual, long-term sustainable changes

Medical Management

Treatment is personalized and may involve dietary restructuring, behavioral support, physical therapy, medical intervention, or bariatric surgery under expert guidance.

Anemia

Blood & Deficiency Condition

Symptoms

  • Chronic fatigue and muscle weakness
  • Pale skin, gums, or inner eye linings
  • Dizziness, lightheadedness & headaches
  • Shortness of breath during light tasks
  • Feeling unusually cold in hands/feet

Prevention & Nutrition

  • Eat iron-rich foods (spinach, lentils, beans)
  • Pair plant iron with Vitamin C for optimal absorption
  • Maintain a varied, balanced daily diet
  • Consult doctors regarding heavy menstrual blood loss

Treatment & Care

Treatment depends on identifying the exact cause (e.g. iron deficiency, vitamin B12 deficiency, chronic blood loss). Iron supplements should be taken under medical supervision.

Emergency First Aid Guide

Immediate actions to stabilize injuries before professional medical services arrive.

Burns

What to Do:

  • Move away from heat source immediately.
  • Cool under cool running water for at least 20 minutes.
  • Remove jewelry or tight items near area (if not stuck to skin).
  • Cover loosely with a clean, non-fluffy dressing or cling wrap.

What NOT to Do:

  • Do NOT apply ice directly to skin.
  • Do NOT apply butter, toothpaste, or oils.
  • Do NOT break or puncture blisters.

Seek Medical Help If:

Burn is large, deep, electrical, chemical, or involves face, hands, feet, genitals, or major joints.

Cuts & Wounds

What to Do:

  • Wash hands thoroughly with soap before treating.
  • Apply gentle, firm pressure with clean gauze/cloth to stop bleeding.
  • Clean wound gently under clean running water.
  • Cover with a sterile dressing or clean bandage.

What NOT to Do:

  • Do NOT probe deep wounds with unsterilized tools.
  • Do NOT scrub deep cut surfaces aggressively.

Seek Medical Help If:

Bleeding is heavy or fails to stop after 10 mins, wound is deep/gaping, or shows redness, heat, and pus (infection).

Sprains & Strains

What to Do:

  • Rest: Avoid putting weight on the injured joint/limb.
  • Ice: Apply a cold pack wrapped in cloth to reduce swelling.
  • Compress: Apply gentle elastic compression bandage if appropriate.
  • Elevate: Prop up injured limb above heart level when resting.

What NOT to Do:

  • Do NOT apply ice directly to bare skin.
  • Do NOT massage or apply heat during first 48 hours.

Seek Medical Help If:

Inability to bear any weight, joint deformity, severe numbness, or pain does not improve after a few days.
